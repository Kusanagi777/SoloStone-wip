D5 Persistent boss damage is save-file state owned by the run layer at PROFILE scope (DungeonRunProfile.BossDamage, per encounter id), because RecordOutcome ends the run on any non-win (rule R10 is kept). A loss to a persistentDamage boss banks its remaining Health; the next run that meets that lord starts it at that Health and in the matching phase; a win clears the record. In-run contribution: ULDA_115 adds 10 carried damage from state.Treasures. The same harvest feeds a RUN-scope state.HeroDamage for ULDA_720 Battle Scars. Runs that do not count for profile progress (the test MajorsCleared already uses) neither read nor write the ledger.
D6 Temporary hero switching is explicit run state state.HeroSwitch (created at the floor's PreMatch, cleared by any non-Fault outcome) and one accessor MatchIdentity(state) that every per-fight reader uses; the run's Class/Power/Deck/Cards are never written by a switch.
D7 Boss decks: 30 cards for every combat-tier encounter (authentic 30-card list as is; short lists and pools are extended to 30 by a fixed rule and recorded as adaptation). Majors, finale bosses and form decks keep their authentic count (10..60). The 30-only checks are relaxed for provenance != solostone only.
D8 Health: encounters use the act curve; "fixedHealth" only for identity Health (Plague Lords 300/200/100, Tekahn 300, Hagatha 100). HealthOffset() must not index BandHealth for band-0 rows: encounters carry an explicit "healthOffset" (default 0; +10 for Dungeon Run finals and nemeses). Recorded once as rule-level adaptation "health-policy".
D9 Anomalies are offered (new Anomaly node: 2 rolled + skip), twists are rolled per act at NewRun; both are stored in state.ActiveRules with a Kind. They are a separate "rules" list in schedule-v1.json, so ModifierCandidates and saved v1/v2 runs are untouched.
D10 Boss-side generated Secrets: per-encounter flag, catalog runs only, with a validator pool check (section 6).
